About BGameWorld

BGameWorld is a small, independent puzzle site. We make free daily games that play in any browser, with nothing to install and no sign-up needed.

What we make

There are eleven games on the site: seven word games (Wordezy, Wordezy Search, Wordezy Scramble, Wordezy Ladder, Warmezy, Letterezy and Lingoezy), three logic puzzles (Sortezy, Linkezy and Mergezy) and a photo trivia game (When & Where). Each one has a guide with its rules and tips.

Every game is designed and built for BGameWorld. The puzzles are generated for the site, and the logic puzzles are checked by a solver so every one can be finished.

How the daily puzzles work

Every game has a daily puzzle that is the same for every player and changes at midnight UTC, so you can compare results with friends wherever they are. The Daily Seven on the homepage picks seven of the games each day for a combined score, and Today's question asks everyone one light question a day.

Streaks, scores and leaderboards are optional. You can play anonymously, or save your account with an email address to keep your progress across devices.

Where the words come from

Our word games use open, public word lists, such as the public-domain ENABLE dictionary, with everyday words chosen for answers and hints. Warmezy's closeness ranks come from openly published word vectors trained on Wikipedia and news text. When & Where uses photographs from archives and public-domain collections.

How the site is paid for

The games are free. BGameWorld is supported by advertising served by Google AdSense. Ads are kept away from the game boards so they never get in the way of play. Our privacy policy explains how advertising cookies work and how to opt out of personalised ads.
