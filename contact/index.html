Contact us

We read every message. The quickest way to reach us is by email.

Email: bgameworld01@gmail.com

What to write to us about

Something isn't working
Tell us which game, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your device and browser (for example, "iPhone, Safari") help us reproduce it.
A word or puzzle looks wrong
Name the game and the date of the puzzle. If a word should or shouldn't be accepted, include the word.
Delete my data
Use the subject "Delete my data" and include the display name you play under, plus the email address if you saved your account. We'll delete your account and scores within 30 days and confirm when it's done. See the privacy policy for details.
Ideas and questions
Suggestions for new games, features or Today's question are welcome. You can also suggest a question directly on the Today's question page.
