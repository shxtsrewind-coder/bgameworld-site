Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

This policy covers bgameworld.com and the games it hosts, such as Wordezy, Sortezy, Warmezy, Mergezy, Letterezy and When & Where. It explains what information we keep, why, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • An anonymous player account. When you play, the game creates one for you automatically, with no name or email needed. It lets us save your progress, streaks and scores.
  • Your email and password, only if you choose to save your account so you can keep your progress across devices. Passwords are handled by our login provider and we never see them.
  • A display name and country, if you set them. These can appear next to your scores on leaderboards.
  • Your game results, such as guesses, scores, times, streaks and anything you create in a game (for example a drawing you submit).
  • Settings stored on your device. Some preferences and progress are kept in your browser's storage.
  • Basic technical data. Like every website, our host receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

How we use it

Only to run the games: saving your progress, showing streaks and leaderboards, picking the daily puzzle, and keeping the site working and secure. We don't sell your personal information.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores player accounts and game data.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Google, through Google AdSense, shows ads on the site. See "Advertising & cookies" below.

Advertising & cookies

BGameWorld shows ads served by Google through Google AdSense, including Google and its partners such as Google Ad Manager. These partners may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure how ads perform.

Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

We don't control these cookies ourselves — they're set directly by Google and its advertising partners, and their own privacy policies explain how they handle your data. See Google's how Google uses information from sites that use our services for details.

If you're in the UK, EU or a similar jurisdiction, you'll be asked for consent before any personalized-ad cookie is set, and you can change your choice at any time from the consent banner.

Our social media apps

BGameWorld uses the Facebook, Pinterest and TikTok developer tools only to publish our own puzzle posts to BGameWorld's own accounts. These apps don't offer a "log in with" option, and they don't collect or store any information about the people who see, like or comment on our posts.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has saved an account with an email address, contact us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything linked to it, send a message to the BGameWorld Page on Facebook saying "Delete my data". Include the display name you play under, and the email address if you saved your account. We'll delete your account, scores and anything you submitted within 30 days and confirm when it's done.

If you never saved your account, your progress is tied only to your browser. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it from your device.

Our social media apps don't store any data about Facebook, Pinterest or TikTok users, so there is nothing to delete there.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
