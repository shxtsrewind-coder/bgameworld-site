How to play When & Where

When & Where shows you a real archival photograph. Study it, then guess the year it was taken, the place on a world map, and the day of the week.

Play When & WhereAll guides

How to play

  1. Look closely at the photo. You can open it full size.
  2. Choose the year you think it was taken.
  3. Drop a pin on the map where you think it was taken.
  4. Pick the day of the week, then submit to see how close you were.

Rules and modes

Scoring
The year is worth up to 5,000 points and the location up to 5,000. The right day of the week adds 1,000, for a best possible score of 11,000.
Daily challenge
One daily photo, the same for everyone, with a leaderboard for the day.
Decade Sort
A second mode: put five photos in order from oldest to newest, as many rounds as you like.

Tips

  • Read the clues in clothing, cars, signs and street furniture. Fashion and vehicles date a photo quickly.
  • Check the photo itself. Black and white, early colour and modern digital each point to different eras.
  • Look for writing. The language and alphabet on a sign narrow the country fast.
  • Weekday clues are rare but real: market stalls, church crowds, or empty office streets can hint at a weekend.

Questions

Where do the photos come from?

From archives and public-domain collections. Each photo is a real historical image.

Do I need to be exact to score?

No. Points are given for getting close, so a near miss on the year or place still scores well.
