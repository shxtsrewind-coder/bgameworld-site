How to play Wordezy Ladder

Wordezy Ladder is a daily word ladder. You start with one four-letter word and climb to another by changing a single letter at a time, and every step on the way has to be a real word.

Play Wordezy LadderAll guides

How to play

  1. Read the start word and the target word.
  2. Change exactly one letter of the current word to make a new real word. For example, COLD can become CORD.
  3. Keep going, one letter per move, until you reach the target.
  4. Try to get there in par, the fewest moves the ladder can be solved in.

Rules and modes

Par
Every daily ladder has a par between 4 and 6 moves. Matching par is the goal; there's no penalty for going over, but the leaderboard ranks fewest moves first.
Undo and hints
Undo is free. A hint fills in the best next word, but it costs an extra move.
Leaderboard
Ranked by fewest moves, then by time. Only your first finish of the day counts.

Tips

  • Compare the start and target letter by letter. Letters that already match can usually stay put.
  • Change vowels early. Swapping the vowel opens up many more real words than swapping a consonant.
  • Work backwards from the target in your head. Knowing the second-to-last word often reveals the whole route.
  • If you're stuck on one letter, change a different letter first and come back to it.

Questions

Do proper nouns count?

No. Every step must be an ordinary English word.

Can I play 5-letter ladders?

The daily is four letters. Unlimited ladders come in four or five letters.
