How to play Linkezy

Linkezy is a dot-connecting puzzle. Join each pair of matching dots with a line, never cross another line, and fill every square on the board.

Play LinkezyAll guides

How to play

  1. Drag from a dot to the other dot of the same colour. Lines move across, up and down, never diagonally.
  2. Lines can't cross or overlap each other.
  3. Every square on the board must be covered by a line.
  4. Connect every pair with the board fully covered to solve it.

Rules and modes

Daily line
One board a day, the same for everyone. It grows from 7×7 on Monday to 11×11 at the weekend.
Levels
100 levels from 5×5 up to 12×12. Each level is the same for every player.
Perfect
Draw each colour in a single move to earn a Perfect badge.
Help when you need it
Undo, restart and hints are always available, and dots can show letters for colour-blind players.

Tips

  • Start with dots on the edges and in corners. They have the fewest routes, so their lines are easiest to place.
  • Hug the walls. Lines that follow the edge of the board rarely block anything else.
  • Look for pairs that sit next to each other. They usually join directly.
  • If a square can only be reached by one colour, that colour has to pass through it.

Questions

Can a line touch another line?

Lines can run alongside each other, but they can't cross or share a square.

Is there only one solution?

Any route counts, as long as it joins every pair and covers every square.
