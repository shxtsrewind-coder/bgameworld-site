How to play Warmezy

Warmezy is a hot-and-cold guessing game played with meaning instead of spelling. There's one secret word a day, and every guess gets a rank that shows how close its meaning is.

Play WarmezyAll guides

How to play

  1. Type any English word and press Enter.
  2. Your guess gets a rank. #1 is the secret word itself, and the lower the number, the closer you are.
  3. Ranks are coloured from boiling (the top 50) through hot, warm and cool to cold (beyond 5,000).
  4. Follow the heat. Keep guessing words related to your best guesses until you hit #1.

Rules and modes

How closeness works
Ranks come from word vectors trained on billions of words of Wikipedia and news text. Words that appear in similar sentences sit close together, so a word can rank high because it means something similar or simply because it shows up in the same kinds of sentences.
Hints
A hint gives you a word about halfway between your best guess and the answer. Hints count as guesses.
Giving up
You can reveal the word at any time. After you solve or give up, you can see the 50 closest words.

Tips

  • Start broad: try very different words such as a food, an animal, a feeling and a place, and see which area is warmest.
  • Think about context, not just synonyms. If "ocean" is hot, try words that sit in ocean sentences, like "wave", "ship" or "deep".
  • Try different forms of a warm word. The plural or the verb form can rank very differently.
  • Once you're in the top 100, slow down. Each guess there is worth thinking about.

Questions

Why is a word with the same meaning ranked low?

The model learns from how words are used, not from a dictionary. Two synonyms that appear in different kinds of sentences can sit far apart.

Does everyone get the same word?

Yes. The secret word is shared by every player and changes at midnight UTC.
