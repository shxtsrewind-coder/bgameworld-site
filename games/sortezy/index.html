How to play Sortezy

Sortezy is a water-sort puzzle. Coloured layers are mixed across glass tubes, and you pour between them until every tube holds a single colour.

Play SortezyAll guides

How to play

  1. Tap a tube to pick it up, then tap the tube you want to pour into.
  2. You can only pour onto the same colour or into an empty tube.
  3. A pour moves as much of the top colour as will fit.
  4. The puzzle is solved when every tube is either full of one colour or empty.

Rules and modes

Daily puzzle
One daily puzzle with 9 colours and 11 tubes, the same for everyone. There's a move target for three stars.
Undo, restart and hints
Undo and restart are free. A hint shows the best next pour and adds 10 seconds to your time.
Levels
Numbered levels grow from 3 colours to 12. Every puzzle is checked by a solver before it's shown, so each one can be solved.
Colour symbols
Turn on symbols to put a shape on each colour if some colours are hard to tell apart.

Tips

  • Keep an empty tube free for as long as you can. It's your most valuable space.
  • Before pouring, check what's underneath the top colour. The move that uncovers a useful colour is often better than the obvious one.
  • Finish one tube at a time. A completed tube is out of the way for good.
  • Plan a few pours ahead near the end. The last colours are where extra moves add up.

Questions

Is every puzzle solvable?

Yes. Every puzzle is solved by the built-in solver before you see it.

What counts as a move?

Each pour is one move, however many layers it carries.
