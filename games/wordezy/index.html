How to play Wordezy

Wordezy is a five-letter word puzzle with one answer a day, the same for everyone. You get six guesses, and every guess tells you which letters are right, which are in the wrong place, and which aren't in the word at all.

Play WordezyAll guides

How to play

  1. Type any real five-letter word and press Enter.
  2. Each tile changes colour. Green means the letter is in the word and in the right spot. Yellow means it's in the word but somewhere else. Grey means it isn't in the word.
  3. Use what you learned to make your next guess. The on-screen keyboard keeps track of every letter you've tried.
  4. Find the word within six guesses to solve the day. If you run out, the answer is revealed.

Rules and modes

One puzzle a day
The daily word changes at midnight UTC, so a player in Lagos and a player in London are solving the same word on the same day.
Practice round
You can also play one practice round a day with a different word. It never counts toward your streak or the leaderboard.
Speed leaderboard
Solved puzzles are ranked by how quickly you found the word. Only your first finish of the day counts.
Streaks
Solve on consecutive days to build a streak. Your statistics show your win rate and how many guesses your wins usually take.

Tips

  • Start with a word that uses common, different letters, such as five letters with two or three vowels. Avoid repeating a letter in your opener.
  • A yellow letter rules out a position as well as confirming the letter. Place it somewhere new on the next guess.
  • Remember that letters can repeat. About a quarter of answers use a letter twice, in words like LEVEL.
  • When several answers fit, spend a guess on a word that tests as many of the uncertain letters as possible, even if it can't be the answer.

Questions

Is there a word list I can see?

No. The answer list is kept on the server so the daily word can't be read from the page. Any common five-letter English word may be the answer.

Can I share without spoiling it?

Yes. The share button copies a grid of coloured squares that shows how you did without showing any letters.
